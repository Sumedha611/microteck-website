Kaveri Microteck Private Limited

Company Profile

Kaveri Microteck Private Limited (KMPL) was started in 2003 with the aim of providing all agricultural inputs in one place for farmers. KMPL is a 100% subsidiary of Kaveri Seed Company Limited, a well-known and trusted name among Indian farmers. KMPL works to support farmers with reliable, quality agricultural products that help to improve crop growth, soil health and farm income.

Introduction

KMPL works to develop and supply safe, eco-friendly agricultural products that help farmers get better crop growth while keeping the soil and environment healthy. Our product range includes Micronutrients, Biostimulants, Biofertilizers and Bio-pesticides, which support crop nutrition and pest control for modern farming. These products are widely used and trusted by farmers across the country. All KMPL products are sold under the “Microteck” brand name and are available through a strong network of dealers and distributors across India, making them easy for farmers to access. With strong scientific research, innovation and a focus on sustainability, KMPL continues to support farmers with high-quality solutions that improve yield, soil health and long-term farm productivity.

Vision

To support farmers with modern and safe agricultural solutions that help them grow better crops, maintain healthy soils and protect nature for a sustainable future.

Mission

To develop safe and eco-friendly farm products that help crops grow better and keep the soil healthy.To provide farmers with reliable, high-quality products backed by science they can trust.To encourage sustainable farming practices that protect land, water and the environment for the future. To ensure our products reach every farmer easily through a strong network of dealers and distributors across India.
